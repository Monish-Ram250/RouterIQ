How it works

01 · Embed

Sentence-BERT turns the prompt into a 384-dimensional representation.

02 · Predict

The trained Residual MLP estimates suitability scores for candidate models.

03 · Route

A cost-aware policy combines suitability and normalised cost to select a candidate.

For accurate comparisons, provide a consistent cost estimate for every candidate model. Candidates with a null cost are treated as unavailable by the API.
